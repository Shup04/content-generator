SAVE 01 — DEEP END
Deep End is a heated water park.
The vending machines keep you fed.
Lock the staff-room door before sleeping.
Something checks the handles every night.
The waterfalls cover its footsteps.
That EXIT sign has no door.

SAVE 02 — SUNROOM LINE
Sunroom Line serves lunch at every stop.
You can sleep on the warm seats.
Board before the bell stops.
The tram never visits a platform twice.
Miss it, and you'll wait there alone.

SAVE 03 — VELVET ORCHARD
Velvet Orchard feeds you for free.
Sleep upstairs, beyond the roots.
Each peach makes your legs a little heavier.
Eventually, you cannot climb the stairs.
The luggage beneath the tree belonged to people who stayed.

SAVE 04 — SIGNAL 03
Signal zero three opens into a school.
Fresh lunches wait inside the lockers.
Take one, then leave before the bell.
The floor disappears when lessons begin.
The footsteps carry on.
