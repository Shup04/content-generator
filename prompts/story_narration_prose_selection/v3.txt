Choose the narration that takes the least effort to understand when heard once.
Rate each from 0 to 1. Imagine the viewer is tired and only half listening.
Prefer one immediately clear place, a simple sense of life there, and one plain
catch, in 3–4 short sentences with common words at roughly grade 3–4 level.

Short words are not enough: lower the rating if a listener has to track a signal,
machine, sequence of actions, technical explanation or several conditions.
Prefer a supported lived consequence over an explanation of how equipment works.
A simple account of life is enough; do not require an incident, a meal, a complete
daily routine or a practical task. Do not reward extra facts or a second warning.

Claims must follow from the supplied world description. Reject invented facts,
misleading simplifications, safety checklists, unexplained terms, poetry and forced
twists. Do not request missing engineering details or more survival coverage.
Use issues for real problems and give a short reason for each rating.
