PHASE 1 — WORLD SIMULATION. Return structured facts, never spoken narration.
Develop the selected concept into a coherent daily survival system. Include:
environment, resource_system, food, water, shelter, inhabitants, threat,
warning_signs, critical_survival_rule, consequence_if_broken, escape_conditions,
daily_routine, long_term_cost and concise causal_logic. Each danger, warning,
protective action and consequence must arise from the environment and its systems.
Explain observable causes and effects. Do not attach arbitrary penalties or curses.
Explain how the player can actually obtain supplies, find shelter and survive a day.
Use null for genuinely unspecified food/water or optional facts; warning_signs may
be empty if there is no detectable warning. Do not invent filler to populate fields. Keep the same single central idea through
the spec. Do not elaborate a simple premise into a system of gauges, warning codes,
rotas, mechanical parts or exceptions. Plain game-world cause and effect is enough;
the origin of an impossible rule may remain unexplained.

Keep approved concept facts EXACTLY in these spec fields:
selected.resource_situation -> resource_system
selected.safe_sleeping -> shelter
selected.inhabitants -> inhabitants
selected.primary_danger -> threat
selected.critical_rule -> critical_survival_rule
selected.rule_consequence -> consequence_if_broken
selected.escape -> escape_conditions
selected.long_term_drawback -> long_term_cost
Refine food, water, warnings, routine and environment consistently with those facts.
If previous_review exists, correct its issues without replacing the selected world.

Also supply the existing structured visual variables; never complete image prompts.
These are separate from the world spec and are not supplied to the narrator.
Cartridge: shell_material_color, shape_language, motifs, object_mood,
hint_scene_description. Use selected.visual_hook as the starting composition for one readable
visual mystery: a familiar place or object with one clearly visible unexplained
detail. Describe the focal subject, the exact strange relationship or placement,
the crop, dominant color, contrasting accent and lighting. Use only facts already
supported by the selected world. Do not invent new mechanics or decorative lore.
If the visual hook is a spatial contradiction, preserve that contradiction when
cropping; do not replace it with a decorative line, an isolated symbol or an
ordinary map fragment. Favor the strongest visible question already in the hook
over a subtler anomaly that needs the story explained before it looks unusual.
The subject must be recognizable on a small cartridge label; the reason it exists
should remain mysterious until the world reveal. Avoid vague fog, generic corridors,
tiny symbols and clues that only work as text. Tease a cropped glimpse, never a full
panorama or the hidden survival bargain. Preserve the selected palette and use a
distinct dominant hue in shell_material_color rather than default smoky plastic.
Environment: environment_description, time_of_day, key_visual_elements, palette,
mood, visual_anomaly, environmental_motion, shot_composition, key_surfaces.
Copy selected.palette, selected.anomaly and selected.time_of_day EXACTLY into
environment.palette, environment.visual_anomaly and environment.time_of_day.
Use restrained old-game motion, simple glow and fog; deterministic visual templates
remain authoritative. Do not add lore or mechanics solely to decorate an image.
