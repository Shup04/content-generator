INDEPENDENT NARRATION REVIEW — CHOOSE A FLOWING TRAVELOGUE, NOT A CHECKLIST.

Review all three candidates against the SAME approved_facts. Rate each candidate ID
exactly once. Do not trust its cited facts, sensory labels or claimed main danger.
The application will select among eligible candidates in this priority order:
1. spoken_flow: most natural when read aloud;
2. not_list_like: least like a sequence of resource/shelter/rule instructions;
3. cliche_free: fewest obvious AI-writing clichés;
4. distinct_fact_count: fewest actual world facts needed to make it interesting.
Scores are 0–1, higher is better. Give a concise rationale explaining the comparison.

Eligibility checks:
- grounded: every claim, including sensory detail and activity, follows from the
  cited approved facts. Valid citations alone do not prove a claim. Reject new
  mechanics, invented sensory attributes or omitted qualifications that change meaning.
- second_person and connected_prose: one natural paragraph addressed to "you".
- checklist_like: true for category-by-category instructions, even joined with "and".
- sensory_detail_count: count concrete sensory/environment details actually in the
  prose; require 1–2. Descriptions do NOT need a practical survival justification.
- ordinary_activity_present: at least one grounded thing the viewer spends time doing.
- distinct_fact_count: require 3–4 actual facts used, not number of fields or citations.
  Do not let a bundled quotation disguise many separate mechanics or instructions.
- main_danger_count: at most ONE main danger/rule. Its warning and consequence can
  belong to that same mechanism; unrelated threats/rules count separately. Zero is fine.
- creepy_asides_or_poetry and forced_twist: reject these when present. A natural
  consequence is not automatically a twist. Do not reward a clever creepy closing line.
- issues: specific factual/style failures, empty only if none. Do not request missing
  resource/shelter/escape information or coverage of every world-spec category.

There is NO practical-line quota, deletion test, required danger ratio, mandatory
unsettling line or demand that every sentence be ominous. Sensory detail and normal
transitions can earn their place by making the travelogue natural and specific.
The world must remain frozen. If all candidates fail, narration alone is rewritten.
