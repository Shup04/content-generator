Review a fictional survival game's internal consistency, not real-world feasibility.

The selected concept defines the world's rules. Strange physics, impossible places,
and unexplained systems are allowed. Their ultimate cause can remain a mystery.
Check whether a viewer can understand what happens, what to do, and why that action
helps within the stated rules. Do not require scientific proof of those rules.

Reject only material story problems:
- Two stated facts contradict each other about the same situation.
- The rule has no understandable connection to the danger, or following it cannot
  help even under this world's stated rules.
- The consequence contradicts the rule or threat.
- Added facts erase the selected cost, change its mechanics, or reverse its tier.
- The described routine depends on a resource the spec explicitly says is unavailable.

Do not reject for missing engineering specifications, equipment ratings, safety
margins, medical evidence, exact quantities, or a complete logistics plan. A working
game-world device does not need a real-world design certification. A stated supply
can sustain the described routine without an inventory calculation. Unspecified
optional facts are not contradictions. Do not invent additional hazards or demand
answers to every possible failure mode.

Assess the spec as a whole. Supporting explanations can be in causal_logic, warnings,
environment, or daily_routine; they need not be repeated inside the critical rule.
Distinguish a recurring permanent burden from a burden that happens every minute.

Score causal_coherence for understandable, consistent game-world cause and effect.
Set practical_system=true when life and protective actions make sense within those
rules. Set preserves_selected_concept based on the selected facts. Put only blocking
contradictions in issues, citing the conflicting facts and the smallest needed fix.
Suggestions for extra detail are not blocking issues. If there are no material
contradictions, use empty issues and a score meeting minimum_coherence. Do not reject
merely because the world is fictional. Do not approve actual contradictions.
