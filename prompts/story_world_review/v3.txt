PHASE 1 — APPROVE THE WORLD SIMULATION, not the writing.
Compare the structured spec against the selected concept. Independently check:
- Does the environment cause the threat?
- Are warnings observable and consistent with how that threat works?
- Does the critical rule actually protect the player?
- Does breaking it logically cause the stated consequence?
- Does the permanent cost emerge from the routine or survival system?
- Are food, water, shelter and the daily routine mutually consistent?
- Is there a plausible reason to live here despite a meaningful downside?
Do not accept an author's causal explanation simply because it asserts a connection.
Check that additions preserve the approved concept and that visual variables do not
invent conflicting facts. A resource_system may concern something other than food;
food and water must describe actual supplies, or be null when unspecified.
Score causal_coherence 0–1, mark preserves_selected_concept and practical_system,
and list concrete issues. Empty issues means no objections, not uncertainty.
Reject weak systems even if their prose or images sound impressive. Failed specs
will be revised; do not lower standards to obtain approval.
