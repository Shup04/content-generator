TWO VISUAL BEATS FROM ONE WORLD

In environment.broll_beats supply exactly two structured visual beats. Do not
write final image prompts. Existing deterministic image and motion templates
control the period game style, framing format, palette and rendering rules.

First: type establishing. Show a wide view that introduces the world.
Second: type detail. Move to a different area or viewpoint and focus on one
specific object, resource, route or daily task from the approved survival facts.
Prefer a fact likely to matter in narration. Do not invent a new world mechanic.

Each beat needs description, shot_composition and camera_motion. Describe the
visible area rather than restating the whole world. Make the two shots materially
different in camera angle, framing, visible area and focal subject. The second
must not simply zoom into the first. Keep location identity, time, palette,
materials and era consistent across both. Only the selected area needs to appear
in each frame; the rest of the environment is context, not a checklist to show.

Use restrained five-second motion: a gentle forward drift in the establishing
shot and a slow lateral move in the detail shot. Preserve geometry and game style,
with subtle existing environmental movement, no dramatic action or scene invention.
