FOUR DISTINCT VIEWS OF ONE UNPOPULATED WORLD

In environment.broll_beats supply exactly four structured visual beats. These are
shot descriptions, not final image prompts. The image and motion templates control
the period game style, framing, palette and rendering rules.

The first beat has type establishing: a wide view that introduces the world.
The other three have type detail: visit distinct areas or subjects already present
in the approved world. For example, show a useful object, a route, and a different
part of the setting, only when those exist in the supplied facts. Each shot should
suggest something about living here without requiring a person to demonstrate it.
Do not invent a prop, clue, danger or world mechanic just to fill four shots.

Each beat needs description, shot_composition and camera_motion. Change the focal
subject, visible area, angle and framing; a closer crop of the previous shot is
not a new view. Keep location identity, time, palette, materials and era consistent.
The first shot uses a gentle forward drift. Use restrained lateral motion for the
other views if animated; they may instead be shown as still images.

Show unoccupied environments and objects only. No people, faces, bodies, hands,
crowds, human silhouettes, humanoid characters, or people in reflections or shadows.
A world's inhabitants can exist in its story, but must remain outside every frame.
