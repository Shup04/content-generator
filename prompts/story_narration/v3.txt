PHASE 2 — NARRATION FROM APPROVED WORLD FACTS ONLY.
Write 4–6 short spoken lines, subject to the supplied narration_policy.
The title is a label, not evidence about the world. approved_facts is the only
source of factual claims. Do not invent, infer, embellish or strengthen facts.
Do not invent times, quantities, supplies, safe places, people or consequences.
Preserve every limiting condition and important qualification when paraphrasing.

Most lines must explain how to live here. Target roughly 70% practical information
and 30% danger/mystery, with only 1–2 unsettling lines and a practical majority.
State how to obtain resources, where to sleep, how to navigate, what daily routine
works, what warning to recognize, or what a rule/consequence means for survival.
Do not describe an image unless that detail affects survival or daily life.
Do not write poetry, creepy closing lines, rhetorical questions or a perfect reveal.
There is no requirement to end with horror or to explain every mystery.
If deleting a line changes nothing practical, remove or replace it. Each line must
change understanding; do not restate the previous line in new wording.

Bad: "That exit sign has no door."
Bad: "The luggage belonged to people who stayed."
Bad: "Each peach makes your legs heavier."
Better, ONLY IF SUPPORTED by approved_facts:
"Ignore the EXIT signs after midnight. They start appearing over walls that weren't doors during the day."
"The bags under the tree are abandoned. Most of your clothes and canned food will come from them."
"The orchard is your only reliable food source. Fruit picked after sunset causes fever, so gather enough before dark."
These demonstrate practical utility. Never import their details into another world.

Return structured lines with text, fact_refs, tone (practical or unsettling), and
practical_change. fact_refs must name the approved fact keys that support EVERY
claim in that line. practical_change briefly explains what the viewer learns to do
or account for; it is review metadata, not spoken text. A decorative description
does not become practical just because it cites an environment fact.
Use direct plain English and "you" naturally. Follow word/line targets without filler.
If previous_attempt exists, repair its unsupported or useless lines using these same
approved facts. Do not revise the world to justify a line.
