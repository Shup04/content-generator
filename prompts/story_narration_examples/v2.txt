DEEP END
Deep End is warm all year, and the pool lights never shut off. Most days you can swim,
explore the upper decks, or search the old snack bars for anything still sealed.
After midnight, something starts walking around the wet parts of the building.
If you hear heavy breathing behind you, keep moving toward carpet and don’t turn around.

SUNROOM LINE
The Sunroom Line never reaches its final station. There is no dining car, so you will
need to search the bins at each stop for leftover food. You can sleep in the front
three carriages. Passengers who sleep in the rear cars sometimes disappear before
morning. If the train stops inside a tunnel, stay in your seat until the sunlight
comes back.
