PHASE 2 — INDEPENDENT FACT AND PRACTICAL-UTILITY CHECK.
Review each draft line in order against approved_facts only. Return one result per
line, numbered from 1. Do not trust its citations, tone label or practical_change.

supported_by_facts: every clause must follow directly from its cited facts. A valid
fact key does not prove the text. Reject new entities, quantities, conditions, times,
instructions or causal claims; also reject contradictions and omitted qualifications
that make a conditional rule sound unconditional. Unknown/null facts are not permission
to fill a gap. The title supplies no additional lore.

changes_practical_understanding: deleting the line would remove useful information
about survival, resources, navigation, shelter, routine, warnings, danger or consequences.
Descriptions of an image, ominous observations and perfect-reveal endings alone fail.
A danger line still needs a practical implication, rather than just a creepy sensation.
repeats_information: true when it restates information already supplied by another line.
tone: classify the actual line as practical or unsettling independently. Only 1–2
lines should be unsettling and practical lines must be the majority (roughly 70/30).
issue: a specific correction when unsupported, impractical, repetitive, poetic or
misclassified; otherwise null. Do not approve a line because it sounds memorable.
Rejected narration is rewritten from the same frozen spec, never repaired by adding lore.
